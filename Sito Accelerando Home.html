We Find the Sound Before the World Does

Accelerando Records is an independent record label and live music promotion company born in UK. We sign bold artists, press real records, and throw nights people don't forget. Raw music. Real stages. No compromise.

Now hosting events in London, Berlin and New York!

About Us

Built on the Belief That Music Changes Everything

Our Story

Accelerando Records was founded by obsessives — people who stayed until the last song, who spent rent money on vinyl, who believed the underground had something the mainstream could never buy. We started as a one-room operation in a London backstreet. We're still in that spirit.

We champion original artists at every stage.

Live events. Record label. Community. That's the whole machine.

We don't sign trends. We sign people who'd make music in a blackout. The sound is the point. Always has been.

Venues

Spaces That Respect the Sound

We only work with venues that care about the music as much as we do. Real acoustics, real atmosphere, real crowds.

🇬🇧 London

Fabwick

Cap. 120

A vibrant cultural hub in the heart of Hackney Wick — part jazz bar, part art gallery, part live music venue. Queen's Yard's most unexpected and essential night-out.

The Roxy

Cap. 200

A subterranean Soho institution. Where the Battle Jam was born. Underground acts, Spotlight Lounge and a crowd that has been chasing real music since the '90s. Wednesday and Friday nights belong here.

T.E. Dingwall Building

Cap. 600

Camden's most storied live music room, beside the lock. Since 1973, it's hosted everyone from The Clash to Coldplay. Industrial, canal-side, legendary.

Barkett

Cap. 100

🇺🇸 New York

Telephone Room

Brooklyn, NY

Hidden behind a red phone booth inside a Bushwick coffee shop, Telephone Room is an intimate, red-lit speakeasy built for close, unforgettable nights: vintage charm, a copper bar, and a stage made for our smaller, more personal shows.
